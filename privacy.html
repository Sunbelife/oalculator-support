隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

数据处理

Oalculator 不收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据。应用无需注册账号，也未集成广告、分析或第三方追踪服务。

设备本地数据

显示模式、打字机的纸等数据都只保存在设备本地。应用不会把这些信息发送给开发者。

系统权限

只有当您在分享面板里选择“存储图像”时，Oalculator 才会请求把图片写入系统相册的权限，它不能读取您的相册。Oalculator 不请求相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录或其他个人数据权限。

删除与保留

卸载应用会移除其本地偏好和游戏数据。系统备份与设备迁移由 Apple 的系统设置管理。

联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送至 Sunbelife@foxmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Data Handling

Oalculator does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data. The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

On-device Data

Data such as your appearance mode and typewriter sheets is stored only on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer.

System Permissions

Oalculator asks for permission to add images to Photos only when you choose Save Image in the share sheet, and it cannot read your photo library. It does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or other personal data.

Deletion and Retention

Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences and game data. System backups and device migration are governed by your Apple system settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Sunbelife@foxmail.com.
