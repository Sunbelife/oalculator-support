隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 9 日

数据处理

Oalculator 不收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据。应用无需注册账号，也未集成广告、分析或第三方追踪服务。

照片、视频与密码

用户主动导入或拍摄的照片和视频保存在设备本地的应用容器中。密码的派生值保存在 Apple 钥匙串中。应用不会把密码、照片、视频或相册信息发送给开发者。

系统权限

  • 相册权限用于导入媒体，以及按用户指令将媒体保存回系统相册。
  • 相机权限用于用户主动触发的拍照和录像。
  • 麦克风权限用于录像时收音。

用户可在 iOS 或 iPadOS“设置”中随时管理这些权限。

删除与保留

用户可在应用内删除媒体。卸载应用会移除其本地应用容器中的内容。系统备份与设备迁移由 Apple 的系统设置管理。

联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送至 Sunbelife@foxmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Data Handling

Oalculator does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data. The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Photos, Videos, and Passcodes

Photos and videos that you choose to import or capture are stored locally inside the app container on your device. A derived representation of your passcode is stored in Apple Keychain. The app does not send your passcode, photos, videos, or album information to the developer.

System Permissions

  • Photo library access lets you import media and save media back to the system library when you request it.
  • Camera access is used for photo and video capture that you initiate.
  • Microphone access is used to record audio with videos.

You can manage these permissions at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Deletion and Retention

You can delete media within the app. Uninstalling the app removes content in its local app container. System backups and device migration are governed by your Apple system settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Sunbelife@foxmail.com.
